Who owns the journey

One accountable human keeps the candidate moving.

Intervention 01Before interviewBrief the candidate on the business, the panel and the round. Not a calendar invite.
Intervention 02Between roundsReconnect and debrief both sides within the day — before doubt sets in.
Intervention 03Before offerRe-test motivation and expectations, and surface counter-offer risk early.
Intervention 04Before joiningStay engaged through the notice period until day one is confirmed.
No handoffs. No "the account manager will get back to you." One name, start to finish.
