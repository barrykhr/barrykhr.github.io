Engagement model

Start with a mandate.

01
Introductory call
What you hire, how you hire, and where it currently breaks.
02
Requirement intake
A working session with the hiring manager — not a forwarded JD.
03
Role calibration
Must-haves, adjacent skills, panel, band and notice expectations agreed.
04
Search plan
Target companies, titles and talent pools confirmed before search starts.
05
Curated submissions
A shortlist with written context, qualified on five dimensions.
06
Interviews
Round-specific preparation, same-day check-ins, debrief both sides.
07
Offer management
Expectations re-tested and counter-offer risk surfaced before the offer.
08
Joining
Engagement across the notice period until day one is confirmed.
One mandate is enough to test the model.
